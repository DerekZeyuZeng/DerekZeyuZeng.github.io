his page records the materials and discussion notes from our upcoming informal fall 2026 reading seminar on sheaf theory. The topic we discussed including the definition of sheaf and Étalé spaces, the manifolds, and sheaf cohomology.

The main focus of the seminar was the book *Manifolds, Sheaves, and Cohomology* by T. Wedhorn. We also consulted parts of the book *Sheaves on Manifolds* by M. Kashiwara and P. Schapira as more advances supplementary metrial.

The seminar was intended as a discussion-driven reading cycle. We worked through definitions, examples, and key constructions together, with an emphasis on understanding the geometrical and topological meaning behind the algebraic machinery.
