These working notes are being prepared as the final project for the Fall 2026 honors topics course on Morse theory taught by Professor Ely Kerman at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. The guiding question is how to recover the homotopy type of a manifold from its critical points and gradient trajectories, retaining information that ordinary Morse homology does not record.

The current draft begins with Morse-theoretic foundations, compactified trajectory spaces, and the construction of the Morse flow category. It then introduces the Cohen–Jones–Segal reconstruction of the manifold through the classifying space of this category, with attention to the Morse–Smale and tameness hypotheses. Later sections outline the stable realization via compatible framings and Pontryagin–Thom maps, generalized Morse homology, and the Atiyah–Hirzebruch spectral sequence. Planned examples and further directions include the attaching map of $\mathbb{CP}^2$, twisted framings and Thom spectra, and connections to Floer homotopy theory.

**Status:** Work in progress. Several proofs and later sections are still outlines.
